Rules that shape the theme:

- Spotify: icon alone only where the full logo will not fit; minimum 21 px; link wording "Play on Spotify" or "Listen on Spotify".
- Apple: the icon is allowed beside other icons of similar shape and size; never alter Apple artwork.
- TIDAL: the diamond alone is for small formats; the word TIDAL must appear beside it (the row label does this).
- SoundCloud and Bandcamp are not fetched yet; neither is in the current catalogue.

The maintainer has the permission that Deezer, YouTube Music, TIDAL, Presto and Boomplay ask for. Anyone else using these marks needs their own.
